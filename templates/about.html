About RaithuConnect

RaithuConnect is a community-driven platform created to empower farmers by connecting them directly with skilled agricultural workers and essential services.

🌾 Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify the way farmers access labor, equipment, and agri-services. Whether it’s a tractor driver, plantation worker, or even a drone spraying expert – we aim to bring help closer to every farmer's field.

💡 What We Offer

  • Search and filter workers by type, location, and availability.
  • Contact workers instantly via WhatsApp or call.
  • Register agricultural workers and dealers easily.
  • Bridge the gap between rural workforce and demand.

👨‍🌾 Who is it for?

This platform is built for:

  • Farmers looking for labor or services
  • Agricultural workers seeking job opportunities
  • Dealers providing agri-equipment or agrochemicals

📍 Local. Simple. Effective.

We’re committed to supporting rural communities by making agricultural hiring smart, accessible, and localized.

Thank you for being part of RaithuConnect – together, we grow stronger.
